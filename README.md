# 🧠 Aptitude & Reasoning Syllabus for All Companies

This comprehensive syllabus covers the **Aptitude, Reasoning, and Verbal** topics commonly asked in placement tests for top companies like **TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Capgemini, Cognizant, IBM, Tech Mahindra, Deloitte, Amazon**, and more.

---

## 🧮 I. Quantitative Aptitude (Mathematics)

### **1. Arithmetic**
- Number System  
- LCM & HCF  
- Divisibility Rules  
- Simplification & Approximation  
- Ratio and Proportion  
- Averages  
- Percentages  
- Profit, Loss and Discount  
- Simple Interest & Compound Interest  
- Time, Speed, and Distance  
- Time and Work  
- Pipes and Cisterns  
- Boats and Streams  
- Mixtures and Alligation  
- Partnership  
- Problems on Ages  

---

### **2. Algebra**
- Basic Algebraic Identities  
- Linear and Quadratic Equations  
- Inequalities  
- Progressions (AP, GP, HP)  
- Functions and Graphs  

---

### **3. Geometry and Mensuration**
- Lines, Angles, and Triangles  
- Circles and Polygons  
- Areas and Perimeters  
- Volumes and Surface Areas (Cubes, Cones, Cylinders, Spheres)  

---

### **4. Data Interpretation**
- Tables  
- Bar Graphs  
- Line Graphs  
- Pie Charts  
- Caselets (word-based data problems)  
- Data Sufficiency  

---

### **5. Probability, Permutation & Combination**
- Basic Counting Rules  
- Permutations  
- Combinations  
- Probability (Events, Mutually Exclusive, Conditional)  

---

## 🧠 II. Logical Reasoning (Analytical Ability)

### **1. Logical Deduction**
- Statement & Conclusions  
- Statement & Assumptions  
- Statement & Arguments  
- Course of Action  
- Cause & Effect  

---

### **2. Puzzles**
- Seating Arrangements (Linear, Circular, Square)  
- Blood Relations  
- Direction Sense  
- Coding-Decoding  
- Syllogism  
- Input-Output  
- Inequalities  
- Logical Sequence of Words  
- Series Completion (Alphabet/Number/Symbol Series)  

---

### **3. Analytical Reasoning**
- Data Sufficiency  
- Decision Making  
- Analytical Puzzles  
- Condition-based Reasoning (e.g., floor, rank, team puzzles)  

---

## 🧩 III. Verbal Ability (English Language)

### **1. Grammar**
- Error Spotting  
- Sentence Correction  
- Fill in the Blanks (Vocabulary & Grammar)  

### **2. Vocabulary**
- Synonyms & Antonyms  
- One Word Substitutions  
- Idioms & Phrases  

### **3. Reading Comprehension**
- Short & Long Passages  
- Inference-based Questions  

### **4. Para Jumbles**
- Rearranging Sentences to form a coherent paragraph
